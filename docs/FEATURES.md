# Features - in detail

## 🏬 Operational Command Center
The landing view shows every location as a live tile: headcount, status pill, role and a
drill-down into that store's staff directory. A stat row summarizes total staff, FMS users,
active systems, branches and overall status.

## ⭐ MIS Performance Scoring
The performance engine grades every employee automatically.
- **Grades A+ → F** from a numeric score, color-coded (green → red).
- **Weekly / Monthly / Yearly** views.
- **Location and department filters**, with per-department averages.
- **Smart alerts:** flags a *3-week decline* and *strong improvements* automatically.
- **Medals** for the top 3 performers per department.
- **CSV export** of any report.
- **Cards or table** view toggle.

## ✂️ Alteration FMS - Live
A real-time view of the tailoring/alteration workflow.
- **Order pipeline:** Received → Cutting → Stitching → Quality → Ready.
- **Auto-refresh** every 30 seconds.
- **Day-wise breakdown** and per-job progress %.
- **On-time vs late** classification with live counters.
- **Location switch** (e.g. main store vs branch).

## 🤝 Task Delegation
- Managers assign tasks with a deadline, priority and responsible person.
- Employees tap **Mark Done** from the dashboard.
- The system computes **On Time / Late** from the deadline automatically and scores it.
- Two-stage flow: employee marks done → manager confirms (final).

## ✅ Daily Checklists
- Per-employee recurring checklists with **time-window logic** (open/upcoming/late).
- Managers can view any employee's checklist; staff see their own.
- Identity-aware: the right person's checklist always opens.

## 👥 HR Dashboard
Live headcount with department and location breakdowns and charts.

## 📈 Marketing & Sales
Revenue analytics with live charts.

## 🗂️ 8 FMS Systems
PO to Document, PO Split, Alteration, Urgent Requirement, Discount, Leave, Salary and SCT -
each linked from the relevant employee profile and from one central place.

## 🎙️ Voice Assistant
Hands-free operation using the browser's Web Speech API:
- "How many staff in *Westside*?"
- "Open MIS" · "Open *Rohit*'s checklist" · "Alteration of *Downtown*"
- "Mark *Meena*'s attendance done" - **reads the task aloud and confirms before acting.**
- Answers org questions (who is the owner / EA / department head).
- English + Hinglish phrasing, works on iPhone / iPad.

## 💬 Messaging & Anonymous Feedback
Internal messaging between staff, plus an **anonymous feedback** channel routed to leadership.

## 🌗 Theme & Responsiveness
One-tap **dark/light** toggle remembered per device. Responsive across desktop, iPad and iPhone.
